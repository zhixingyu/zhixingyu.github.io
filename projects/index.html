    Projects

    Here is an overview of the key research projects I have led or contributed to.

    1. Multimodal Representation Learning for Affective Computing

    • Role: Project Lead
    • Focus: Multimodal Sentiment Analysis (MSA) and Emotion Recognition.
    • This project focuses on representation learning for multimodal affective computing. The goal is to develop robust learning frameworks that can effectively integrate heterogeneous modalities, including text, audio, and visual signals. The project addresses several key challenges in multimodal learning, such as modality gaps, cross-modal semantic inconsistency, noisy modalities, uncertain emotional boundaries, and imbalanced modality contributions.
    • I developed a series of multimodal representation learning methods, including fuzzy attention fusion, multi-view cross-modal fusion, fine-grained tri-modal interaction, direction-aware feature recalibration, multi-scale semantic recovery, and structured latent emotional modeling. These methods aim to improve the robustness, complementarity, and interpretability of multimodal affective representations.

    2. EEG-based Emotion Recognition and Attention Modeling

    • Role: Core Member
    • Focus: Brain Signal Representation Learning and Game-based Interventions.
    • This project investigates learning-based modeling for EEG signals, with emphasis on affective and cognitive state recognition. The research aims to explore how temporal, spatial, and relational patterns in EEG signals can be represented for emotion recognition, attention assessment, and game-based intervention. The project involves interdisciplinary collaboration and combines experimental design, signal preprocessing, feature extraction, brain network construction, and deep learning-based recognition models.
    • My work mainly focuses on EEG data analysis, representation learning, and model development. I contributed to the design of experimental protocols, preprocessing pipelines, and evaluation procedures. I also participated in exploring channel feature selection, dynamic brain network modeling, and deep learning methods for robust EEG-based recognition.

    3. Human Activity Recognition and Assessment with Wearable Sensors

    • Role: Core Member
    • Focus: Sensor-based Movement Analysis and Health Assessment.
    • This project focuses on human activity recognition and movement assessment using wearable and contact sensors, including IMU and pressure sensor data. The research aims to develop learning-based methods for modeling multi-source sensor signals and understanding human movement patterns. Application scenarios include children’s action recognition and assessment, abnormal gait recognition, and health-related movement analysis.
    • My work includes supporting experiment protocol design, analyzing IMU and pressure sensor data, and developing learning models for activity recognition and movement assessment. I also guided master’s students in data preprocessing, model implementation, result analysis, and paper preparation.
